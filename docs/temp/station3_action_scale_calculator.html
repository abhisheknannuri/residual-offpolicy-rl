Action scale calculator, Station 3

Set agent.actor.action_scale per joint and see what the residual can do in real units: radians per tick, per ACT chunk, and millimetres at the gripper, next to ACT's own step sizes from the training data. Numbers come from the delta dataset (55,177 frames), the RL scaler and the step-70016 eval logs.

How the numbers are computed

  1. Exact. The box half-width is he = half · (1 + s), with half fixed by the dataset (floored at 0.05). The residual's real size per tick is r · he, so the cap is s · (1 + s) · half. The helper solves s² + s − k·median/half = 0.
  2. Exact. Corrected ACT step = ACT's median step + cap, the same as unscale(clip(scale(a) + s)) while the ±1 clip is inactive. It was inactive on 100% of eval ticks.
  3. Upper bound. Per chunk = 14 ticks × cap. ACT replays 14 actions open-loop and nothing in delta control undoes a deviation inside a chunk. At eval the residual kept one sign for the whole chunk in 56-82% of chunks.
  4. Measured estimate. Realized per chunk = β × chunk bound, where β is how much of a commanded residual radian the follower actually moved. β comes from a per-joint fit on the step-70016 evals (R² shown on each card). Joint 4's fit is poor and should not be relied on.
  5. Measured estimate. mm at the gripper = rad × the joint's gripper sensitivity, fitted from the dataset's joint angles and gripper positions (R² 0.96-0.997). It is shown as a range over arm poses, for that joint moving alone. Joint 5 is the wrist roll and barely moves the gripper position.
  6. Noise. Exploration noise is N(0, stddev) in the same normalized units, so its real 3σ is 3 · stddev · he. Compare it with the cap: noise much larger than the cap means the critic mostly sees noise on that joint.
Before running with a new action_scale: action_scale changes he, and so changes every normalized action stored in the replay buffers. It is not part of either buffer cache key yet. Add it to both keys, or move the old caches away, so old buffers are not reused with the wrong box.

These step statistics cover the whole task. For insertion-only RL, recompute half and the median step from the insertion frames: the steps there are smaller, so the same s gives a larger cap relative to ACT.
